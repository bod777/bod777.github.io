Mental maths is like the gym. Nobody is just good at it; you do your reps, and you keep getting better. The idea came from work and a colleague.

You get a big sum and guess quickly. Ballpark tells you whether you landed within 10, 5 or 2 percent, then shows you the shortcut that would have got you there in your head. It keeps a record of your accuracy and speed, so you can watch yourself improve.
