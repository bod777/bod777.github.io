I used to track spending with a Google Form and a spreadsheet, entering twenty expenses at once from a bank statement. That's how "Tesco" and "Tescos" became two different shops.

Now picking a suggestion fills in the payee, amount and category in one tap, and a warning shows up before I save something I've already logged. I brought three years of old spreadsheets into it too.
