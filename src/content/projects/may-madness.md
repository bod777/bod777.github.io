I host a Met Gala night every year. This year we voted instead of just arguing: 255 looks from the 2026 Met Gala in March Madness-style brackets, head to head, until one was left.

There's a draft mode where everyone picks their own top 32, and Queen of the Gala, where one look holds the crown until someone knocks it off. I built it in two days.
