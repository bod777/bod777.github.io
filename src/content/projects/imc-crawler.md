My brother wanted to understand how our local cinema decides what to show. So a crawler checks the seat map of every showing, and a dashboard lays the day out screen by screen: which films are selling, which are playing to nearly empty rooms, and what sold best.

It's still the project I add to most. The most recent change lays each seat map out the way the room actually is.
